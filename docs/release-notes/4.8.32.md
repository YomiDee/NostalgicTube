# NostalgicTube 4.8.32 (beta)

- Hide original YouTube home and watch recommendations from document start to prevent a first-load flash. Restore the original feed when disabled.
- Hide original watch recommendations and category chips while the replacement feed is still loading.
- Add a compact dark calendar with month/year controls, a close button, automatic saving and a maximum year that follows the computer year. Selecting a date keeps the calendar open.
- Expand discovery to 480 terms in eight languages and 1,920 combinations; avoid the preceding 256 queries.
- Mix categories and languages, interleave discovery sources and show at most two results per search in pure Random mode (up to 12 per batch).
- Recognize compact English ages such as “9y ago” without relaxing ambiguous-date exclusions.

Random discovery uses varied YouTube searches, not a uniform sample of all YouTube videos. No online AI service or API key is required.

Update: extract the ZIP and replace the contents of your existing NostalgicTube installation folder. Reload the extension in brave://extensions (chrome://extensions or edge://extensions for those browsers), then reload YouTube. Do not uninstall if you want to keep your local interests and settings.

Verification: 25 automated tests pass. Real-browser confirmation of the first-load flash fix is still pending. The experimental autoplay changes are not included.
